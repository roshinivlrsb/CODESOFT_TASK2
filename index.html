01 — UNDERSTAND

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs or financial details.

📧

Phishing Emails

Fake emails designed to make you click malicious links or open harmful attachments.

🌐

Fake Websites

Websites that imitate legitimate services to steal usernames, passwords or other information.

📱

Smishing

Phishing attacks delivered through SMS or messaging applications.

02 — IDENTIFY

Spot the Warning Signs 🔍

⚠️ Urgent Messages

Messages that pressure you to act immediately.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Links with unusual domains or misspelled website names.

👤 Unknown Sender

Emails or messages from unexpected or unfamiliar senders.

🔑 Password Requests

Legitimate organizations generally do not ask for your password through email or messages.

📎 Unexpected Attachments

Be careful with files you were not expecting.

🎁 Too Good to Be True

Fake prizes, rewards and offers can be used as bait.

03 — SOCIAL ENGINEERING

How Attackers Manipulate People 🧠

⏰

Urgency

Creating pressure so victims act without thinking.

😨

Fear

Threatening account suspension or other consequences.

🎁

Rewards

Offering fake prizes, discounts or rewards.

🎭

Impersonation

Pretending to be a bank, company, teacher or friend.

04 — PROTECT YOURSELF

Stay Safe Online 🛡️

✅ Verify the sender before responding.
✅ Check website addresses carefully.
✅ Never share passwords or OTPs.
✅ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
✅ Avoid clicking unexpected links or attachments.
✅ Report suspicious messages to the appropriate service.

05 — TEST YOUR KNOWLEDGE

Quick Phishing Quiz 🧩

You receive an email saying: "Your account will be blocked soon. Click here immediately."

What should you do?

STOP → THINK → VERIFY → CLICK

A few seconds of caution can protect your personal information.
